How to use Chat Gator

Chat Gator is a free damage meter and log parser for Dark Age of Camelot, including the Eden server. It reads your game's chat.log and shows your damage, healing, realm points, spells, styles, pets and defense, fight by fight. It all runs in your browser, with nothing to install.

Getting started

How do I turn on chat logging?

In game, type /chatlog or press Ctrl+L. The game starts writing everything in your chat window to chat.log. Doing it again stops logging, and logging also stops when you log out, so turn it on each time you play.

Where is my chat.log file?

On Windows it's in your Documents folder:

Documents\Electronic Arts\Dark Age of Camelot\chat.log

On Linux with Wine or Lutris it's the same path inside the Wine prefix, for example ~/.wine/drive_c/users/<you>/Documents/Electronic Arts/Dark Age of Camelot/chat.log.

How do I load it?

Open Chat Gator and drag your chat.log onto the page, or click Choose log or session file. Want to see what it looks like first? Pick a real sample session (a Bainshee or a Minstrel) and click Try a sample log.

Can it update live while I play?

Yes, in Chrome or Edge. Choose your chat.log and keep the tab open: Chat Gator re-reads the file every few seconds, so your numbers, cooldowns and enemy list keep up as you play. If the page is refreshed, it offers to pick up where you left off.

What Chat Gator shows

  • Overview: damage done and taken, healing done and received, realm points, deathblows and deaths, with a chart of the whole session.
  • Damage, Healing, Defense: who you hit and who hit you, who you healed, and your blocks, parries, evades, ablatives and bladeturns.
  • Spells & Styles: every spell, style and song with hits, crits, resists and how often it landed, plus a timeline dot for each use, with crits and deathblows marked.
  • Pets: each pet's melee, spells and damage-add separately, with damage over time.
  • Realm Points: RP by source, including kills, battle ticks, support activity, keep and tower captures, activities and placements.
  • Resurrects and cure mezz: who you rezzed and demezzed, and who rezzed you.

Your log is split into fights automatically. Drag across any chart to focus every tab on just that stretch of time. Type @tag something in chat during play to drop a labelled marker on the charts.

Questions

Does it work on Eden?

Yes. Chat Gator is built and tested on real Eden logs, including Eden's battle ticks, activity rewards (Hold, Collection, Patrol Hunt…) and placement realm points.

Is my log uploaded anywhere?

No. Your log is read and parsed inside your browser tab and never leaves your computer. The only thing sent is one small note when you start a session (your character's name, class and realm, if known) so we can count how many sessions people start. Bask group sessions share combat numbers only, never log lines or chat.

What is Bask?

Bask is a live group view for up to 8 players. Everyone runs Chat Gator on their own log, and the group sees each other's damage, healing, resists, interrupts, pets, cooldowns and deathblows side by side. A bask is deleted after 12 hours with no activity.

Why doesn't my realm point or healing total match /stats?

/stats counts your whole login, including anything earned before you turned logging on, so its realm points can be higher than what's in your log. For healing it's the other way round: the game's HP healed only counts some of your heals, while Chat Gator counts every heal in the log.

My chat.log is huge. Can I clear it?

Yes. While watching your log live, open the ⋯ menu and choose Clear log file. Chat Gator can save a timestamped backup first, named after your character.

Can I share a session?

Yes. Use Download snapshot from the ⋯ menu and send the file; anyone can drop it into Chat Gator to see the same breakdown.
