About Chat Gator

Dark Age of Camelot writes everything in your chat window to a file called chat.log when you turn logging on with /chatlog. Chat Gator reads that file and turns it into the numbers the game doesn't show you: how much damage and healing you did and took, which spells and styles landed or were resisted, what your pets did, and where your realm points came from.

Your log is split into fights automatically, and every chart can be zoomed to a single moment. In Chrome or Edge it can follow your log while you play, so the meters and the cooldown tracker update as you go. It's built and tested on real logs from the Eden server and reads the standard DAoC log format.

Everything is worked out inside your browser, so your log never leaves your computer. Playing in a group? Bask lets up to eight players see each other's combat numbers side by side. New to logging? The how to use guide walks through turning it on and finding the file.
