What Chat Gator does

  • Damage, healing & realm point meters — broken down by spell, style, weapon, attacker, healer, or pet.
  • Fight-by-fight splitting — your log is automatically broken into individual engagements, or view your whole session's totals at once.
  • Ability cooldown tracker — the first time you cast a spell, style, or realm ability with a known cooldown, it gets a permanent spot in a tracker shown on every tab: full brightness and "Ready" when it's up, dimmed with a shrinking bar and countdown the moment you use it. Live, but not quite real-time — the game client buffers lines before writing them to the log file, so there can be a delay (shown separately as "File checked" vs. "Last updated" while watching) before a cast actually shows up here.
  • /bg center callouts stay on screen on every tab, so you don't miss one that already scrolled past.
  • Watch your chat.log live while you play (Chrome/Edge) — stats update in near real time, nothing to re-upload or re-drop.
  • Clear your log file from the browser when it's gotten big, with an optional timestamped backup first.
  • Bask with your group — start a live group session for up to 8 players: everyone's damage, healing, resists, interrupts, pets, cooldowns and deathblows in one shared view. Combat numbers only, no chat, gone after 12 hours with no activity.
  • Share a session as a file — a groupmate or BG leader can drop it in and see the same breakdown.
  • Your own in-game /stats totals (RP, deathblows, deaths, kills/death) shown alongside the computed numbers, when available.
  • 100% local — your log is parsed in this browser tab and never leaves your machine.
