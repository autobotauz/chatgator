Gather your gators

A bask is a shared view for up to 8 players. Each player's Chat Gator reads their own chat.log live and shares only combat numbers: damage and healing in and out (and who it went to or came from), resists, interrupts, pets, cooldowns and deathblows. No chat, no raw log lines, no ability breakdowns, no realm points. A bask and everything in it is deleted after 12 hours with no activity.

    🧼 Tip: start with a clean chat.log

    Bask reads your whole log when you join, so a log that's grown for weeks makes joining slower. Before a session:

    1. Find chat.log in your DAoC folder (usually Documents\Electronic Arts\Dark Age of Camelot). Copy it somewhere first if you want to keep it, then delete it.
    2. In game, type /chatlog (or press your chat log macro). A fresh, empty chat.log appears.
    3. Join the bask and pick that new file.

    Watching your log on the main Chat Gator page? Its Clear log button does steps 1–2 for you, with an optional backup.

    Live baskets aren't set up on this copy of Chat Gator yet (no database key in js/bask-config.js). Saved bask files still open fine.
