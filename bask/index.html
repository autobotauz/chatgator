Sharing live

Gather your gators

A bask is a shared view for up to 8 players. Each player's Chat Gator reads their own chat.log live and shares only combat numbers: damage and healing in and out (and who it went to or came from), resists, interrupts, pets, cooldowns and deathblows. No chat, no raw log lines, no ability breakdowns, no realm points. A bask and everything in it is deleted 48 hours after its invite code is created.
