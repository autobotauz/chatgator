Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Chat Gator is a free, fan-made tool for Dark Age of Camelot players, run by one independent developer. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Your chat.log stays on your computer

When you open or drop a chat.log into Chat Gator, it is read and parsed inside your browser tab. The log file and the lines in it are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What Chat Gator sends

When you start a new session from your own log, Chat Gator sends one small note to our database: your character's name, class and realm (if they can be worked out from the log) and the time. We use it only to count how many sessions people start, which is shown as the "started today" numbers on the home page. Trying a sample log, opening a shared snapshot, or reloading the page does not send this note.

Bask group sessions

If you create or join a bask, the character name you enter and your combat numbers (damage and healing done and taken, resists, interrupts, pets, cooldowns, deathblows, and any @tag text you type) are shared with the other players in that bask through our database. Chat text and raw log lines are never shared. A bask and everything in it is deleted automatically after 12 hours with no activity.

The database is hosted by Supabase. Like most web services, it may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for a short time for security and reliability.

Stored in your browser

Chat Gator saves a few things in your own browser so the site works the way you left it: your settings (such as icon style and hidden idle time), your recent baskets, and your current session so it can be picked up again after a refresh. This data stays on your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Spell names

To show spell and realm ability details, Chat Gator may download a public spell list from the Eden character planner (eden-daoc.net). Nothing about you or your log is sent with that request.

To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), you're asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

Chat Gator is made for players of Dark Age of Camelot and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them.
